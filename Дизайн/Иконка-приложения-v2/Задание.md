## Отправленный промпт

Use case: precise-object-edit. Edit target: existing Sudoku app icon.
One production-ready square full-bleed opaque 1024x1024 icon, no device mockup.
Preserve EXACT 3x3 grid and five digits in these cells: top row [empty,3,empty],
middle row [1,4,empty], bottom row [empty,8,6]. Large crisp elegant serif
numerals, optically centered, dark espresso on warm light ivory parchment.
Outer frame dramatically thinner: elegant single sepia stroke about 10px;
grid lines about 7px. Refined clarity, balanced negative space, subtle paper,
nearly flat restrained material treatment, no thick bevel, deep shadow,
gloss, noisy grunge, additional objects/text/logos. Full square opaque canvas,
no premasked transparent rounded corners; OS will mask the icon. Exact
numerals order, high readability at small size. One icon only.

Метод: встроенный image_gen; CLI/API fallback не использовался.
