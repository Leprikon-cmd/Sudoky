## Промпт

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE designer comparison/contact sheet, NOT production individual assets and NOT an app screenshot. Create a polished proposal board with EXACTLY 12 different hand-painted icon ideas: 4 columns x 3 rows. Pale warm rice-paper background #F7F0DC, very quiet paper grain; generous space, all icons equally sized and aligned. Every icon must be enclosed in the SAME OPEN ENSO BRUSH CIRCLE, softly irregular dry-brush ink border, flat, no button tile, no 3D. Reference Image 1 is only the ring style and thickness reference; reference Image 2 is only the recognizable sage identity for one portrait icon. Use muted warm sepia #806C4E for borders and pictograms, restrained olive #89905A only as a small accent if needed. Symbols dark enough to be clear inside the ring, separated from circle with generous margin; practical readable simple silhouettes at 40-48pt, low detail, not tiny ornamental illustration. NO books or parchment-scroll symbols, since those current icons were rejected. No landscape backgrounds inside circles, no filled glossy disks.

Layout: 4 columns headed with these exact Russian labels outside the icons: "Продолжить путь", "Новый путь", "Уровни познания", "О проекте". Split long headings into two lines if needed, elegant readable sepia serif. Three rows with a small row marker at the left "1", "2", "3". Do not add any other labels or slogans. Each circle corresponds to this exact matrix, left to right:

Row 1, clear universal symbols:
(1,1) one simple right-pointing PLAY TRIANGLE inside brush circle, resume game.
(1,2) one clear PLUS symbol inside brush circle, begin new game.
(1,3) simple THREE VERTICAL BARS increasing in height with a small baseline inside brush circle, statistics (NOT a mountain).
(1,4) classic lowercase information "i", dot and stem in serif style, inside brush circle, about the project.

Row 2, more connected to the game:
(2,1) tiny simplified 3x3 Sudoku grid with 3 filled sepia cells and a small solid play triangle on its lower-right corner, already-started puzzle continue.
(2,2) simple EMPTY 3x3 Sudoku grid with a clear plus at its upper-right corner, new puzzle.
(2,3) simple UPWARD LINE GRAPH with three connected dots and an L-shaped axis, statistics.
(2,4) simplified ink-and-watercolor HEAD AND SHOULDERS PORTRAIT of the same elderly bald white-bearded sage from reference2, recognizable calm face, not the full seated figure, about the game/author.

Row 3, expressive alternatives:
(3,1) one bold RIGHTWARD ARROW with a short straight shaft, continue forward; must not be a circular reload arrow.
(3,2) one small TWO-LEAF SPROUT with short stalk and no pot, a new beginning.
(3,3) one simple TROPHY CUP with two handles and a short pedestal, record of results; no crown/stars/laurels.
(3,4) one QUILL PEN with a very small INK POT, authorship/credits; no text/scroll/book.

Make the circle geometry and optical weight coherent across all 12 icons. Keep circles 1:1 round, no stretching. No collisions between subjects and border, no cropped icons. Overall landscape sheet about 1800x1500 pixels, all 4 columns and all 3 rows fully visible. This is a design exploration for owner choice only, not finalized implemented UI.
